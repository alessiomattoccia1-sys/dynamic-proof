# Limitations

- **Physical domain.** The laboratory checks perturbative local anomaly cancellation in an isolated U(1) sector of left-handed Weyl fermions. Vacuum consistency, the scalar potential, radiative masses, higher-dimensional operators, Green–Schwarz cancellation, and nonperturbative effects are outside the model.
- **Rank.** Vacuum expectation values are assumed nonzero, with independent coefficients for the allowed symmetric matrix entries. Generic rank establishes existence within that parameter space. A scalar potential or an additional symmetry can impose further relations.
- **Finite completeness.** UNSAT applies to the declared charges, constraints, and scalar pool. With unrestricted scalar charges, the finite pool exhausts those that can contribute matrix entries. Global sign equivalence assumes access to the field and its conjugate. Greatest common divisor normalization applies when no other physical charges fix the scale.
- **Budget.** Reports distinguish timeout, unknown status, model limits, and incomplete enumeration. For large supports, the determinant can remain in exact unexpanded form alongside a rational witness with verified rank. Budget-limited runs remain in the registry.
- **Formalization.** Lean verifies the odd one-scalar case and its generalization to odd multiples. The two-label threshold has an analytic derivation and a finite cross-check. Divisibility by four has two analytic proofs. Formalization of these last two results remains open.
- **Priority.** The literature assessment covers the recorded sources and findings. A Level C original candidate also requires relevant comparisons with the literature and independent checks in the core.
