# Prior art and novelty

The primary literature establishes the anomaly conditions and earlier work on mass generation. The registry records source provenance and locators.

- **Costa, Dobrescu, and Fox (2019)**, equations (1)–(2) and parametrization. They give a general solution of the U(1) anomaly equations. The laboratory's bounded counts verify finite instances of those equations. PT-003 and PT-007 additionally constrain the mass-matrix support and the scalar charges. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.123.151601) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.13729) · [INSPIRE](https://inspirehep.net/literature/1737609)
- **Costa, Dobrescu, and Fox (2020)**, sections II, III, and IV.C. They prove the five-Weyl-fermion minimum for chiral U(1) and give a five-charge solution. Their Table II mass example has two U(1) factors, six fermions, one scalar, and three Dirac masses. PT-003 considers a single U(1) and symmetric matrices with possible Majorana entries. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.101.095032) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11991)
- **Berryman, de Gouvêa, Hernández, and Kelly (2016)**, section IV and Figure 2. They study minimum scalar counts for Dirac masses and allow a remaining massless mode. This is the closest precedent among the sources examined for PT-002, PT-003, and PT-007. Those studies ask whether the entire symmetric matrix for a specified U(1) support is invertible. Equivalence to other universal formulations remains open. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.94.035009) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.03610)
- **Batra, Dobrescu, and Spivak (2006)**, section 2, construct integer extensions of chiral sets to anomaly-free sets. **Allanach, Gripaios, and Tooby-Smith (2020)** give an alternative geometric parametrization. Both are references for the charge-class checks. [Batra et al.](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2222081) · [Allanach et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP05(2020)065)

PT-001, PT-002, and PT-008 are reproduction checks. For PT-003, the open bibliographic question concerns the odd-multiples constraint for matrices admitting Majorana terms. For PT-007, it concerns the threshold \((a+b)/\gcd(a,b)\) for the scalar-label relation and the associated mass constraint.

PT-011 requires \(4\mid n\) for full mass rank when diagonal terms are allowed and at least one fixed point \(h/2\) or \(-h/2\) is absent. The proof uses the two charge reflections to derive the constraint even when Majorana masses are possible. PT-012 gives an eight-fermion witness in the same U(1). Priority of the constraint and threshold remains open. [Berryman et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.94.035009) · [Costa et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.101.095032)
